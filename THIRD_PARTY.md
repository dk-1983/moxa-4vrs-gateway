# Third-party and vendor materials

English · [Русский](THIRD_PARTY.ru.md)

The MIT License applies to original 4VRS Gateway code and documentation
unless a file says otherwise. It does not relicense third-party material.

Moxa firmware, toolchains, SDK/example archives, libraries, headers,
documentation, and binaries remain subject to Moxa's licenses and terms.
Proprietary binaries copied from devices must not enter a public repository
without established redistribution rights.

This project records provenance, versions, and cryptographic hashes needed for
reproducible local setup. Obtain required vendor artifacts separately from an
authorized official source and keep them in ignored `vendor/` staging.

Before public release, tracked historical `_legacy_materials/` and
`_live_reference/` binary evidence must be removed from public Git history or
redistribution rights must be established. Original forensic conclusions may
remain without redistributing those binaries.

Historical manuals, SDK/example trees, firmware, utilities, and other Moxa CD
materials under `_legacy_materials/` are local forensic references. Their
presence does not establish redistribution permission. `docs/references.md`
indexes their provenance and authority; a public release should link to an
official vendor source where available and must not include local copies unless
redistribution rights are explicitly established.
